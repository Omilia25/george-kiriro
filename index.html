01

Discovery & Story Concept

We clarify your brand objective, target audience, core message, and determine the optimal format (vertical reel, commercial, or feature).

02

Production & 4K Filming

On-location or studio shooting using high-end cinema gear, wireless lavalier audio capture, professional lighting, and guided interviews.

03

Post-Production & Sound

Story assembly, broadcast-grade color grading, kinetic captions, B-roll overlays, and Adobe Audition sound design for maximum retention.

04

Delivery & Launch Advice

Final full-resolution export across all desired aspect ratios (9:16 vertical and 16:9 widescreen), plus advice on captioning and distribution.
